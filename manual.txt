SECTION 1: PRODUCT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The EcoSport X is EcoRide's flagship smart electric scooter, engineered for urban commuters
who demand performance, reliability, and connectivity. It combines a high-torque brushless
motor with an intelligent battery management system (BMS) and a companion mobile app for
real-time diagnostics.

Model Variants:
- EcoSport X Standard  : 36V / 10Ah battery, top speed 25 km/h, range up to 40 km
- EcoSport X Pro       : 48V / 15Ah battery, top speed 35 km/h, range up to 65 km
- EcoSport X Pro+      : 48V / 20Ah battery, top speed 35 km/h, range up to 90 km

Scooter Weight        : 14.5 kg (Standard), 16.2 kg (Pro), 17.8 kg (Pro+)
Max Rider Weight      : 120 kg
Ingress Protection    : IP54 (splash-resistant, not waterproof)
Tire Type             : 10-inch self-sealing pneumatic tires
Brakes                : Dual disc brakes with regenerative braking on rear wheel
Display               : 3.5-inch LCD with speed, battery level, trip distance, and mode

SECTION 2: BATTERY & CHARGING
================================================================================

Battery Type          : Lithium-ion (LiPo cells, Grade A)
Charging Input        : 100–240V AC, 50/60Hz universal adapter
Standard Charge Time  : 4–5 hours (0% to 100%) for Standard model
Pro Charge Time       : 5–6 hours (0% to 100%) for Pro and Pro+ models
Fast Charge (optional): 2.5–3 hours using the EcoRide Fast Charger accessory (sold separately)
Battery Lifespan      : Rated for 800 full charge cycles before capacity drops below 80%
Operating Temperature : 0°C to 45°C. Do not charge below 0°C or above 45°C.

Battery Care Tips:
- Avoid storing the scooter at below 20% charge for extended periods.
- For long-term storage (>2 weeks), maintain charge between 40–60%.
- Do not use third-party chargers. EcoRide is not liable for battery damage caused by
  non-certified charging accessories.
- The battery is non-removable on Standard models. Pro and Pro+ batteries can be
  detached using the EcoRide Battery Key (included in Pro/Pro+ box).

Charging Indicator Lights (on charger brick):
- Red solid     : Charging in progress
- Green solid   : Fully charged
- Red flashing  : Charging error — check cable connection or contact support

SECTION 3: GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

Unboxing Checklist:
- EcoSport X scooter (folded)
- Charger adapter (regional plug)
- EcoRide Battery Key (Pro/Pro+ only)
- Quick Start Card
- Reflective ankle strap
- This manual

Initial Setup Steps:
1. Unfold the scooter and click the stem into place until you hear a firm click.
2. Charge the scooter fully before first use.
3. Download the EcoRide app (iOS / Android) and create an account.
4. In the app, tap "Add Scooter" and scan the QR code on the base of the deck.
5. Register your scooter with your purchase receipt to activate the warranty.

Riding Modes:
- Eco Mode   : Top speed limited to 15 km/h. Best range. Recommended for beginners.
- Standard   : Top speed 25 km/h (Standard) or 35 km/h (Pro/Pro+).
- Sport Mode : Instant torque boost. Higher power draw. Available on Pro/Pro+ only.

To switch modes, double-tap the power button while riding. The display will flash the
current mode name.

SECTION 4: TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Scooter does not turn on:
- Ensure battery is charged above 5%. A deeply discharged battery may show no response
  for up to 60 seconds after plugging in the charger.
- Check that the stem is fully clicked into the locked position — a safety interlock
  prevents the motor from activating if the stem is loose.
- Perform a hard reset: hold the power button for 10 seconds until the display flashes.
- If the issue persists, the BMS may have triggered a protection shutdown. Connect to
  the EcoRide app — the "Diagnostics" tab will show the fault code.

Motor cuts out while riding:
- This typically indicates thermal throttling. The motor automatically reduces power
  above 65°C to prevent damage. Stop riding, park in shade, and wait 10–15 minutes.
- Check for error code E03 on the display. E03 indicates motor overcurrent — usually
  caused by riding on steep inclines at full throttle for extended durations.

Battery drains faster than expected:
- Cold weather significantly reduces effective range. At 5°C, expect 20–30% less range
  than the rated figure.
- Riding consistently in Sport Mode on Pro/Pro+ reduces range by up to 35%.
- Check tire pressure. Under-inflated tires increase rolling resistance and drain the
  battery faster. Recommended pressure: 45–50 PSI.
- If capacity loss exceeds 20% within the first 12 months, contact support — this may
  be covered under the battery warranty.

Brakes feel spongy or unresponsive:
- Disc brake pads wear over time. Inspect pad thickness every 3 months or 500 km,
  whichever comes first. Minimum pad thickness before replacement: 1.5 mm.
- If the rear regenerative brake feels inconsistent, recalibrate it via the EcoRide app
  under Settings > Brake Calibration.
- Do not ride if brakes feel completely unresponsive. Contact support immediately.

Error Codes Reference:
- E01 : Low battery — charge immediately
- E02 : Over-voltage — stop charging, allow battery to cool
- E03 : Motor overcurrent — rest for 15 minutes, reduce throttle on inclines
- E04 : BMS communication failure — hard reset required
- E05 : Throttle sensor fault — do not ride, contact support
- E06 : Brake sensor fault — do not ride, contact support

SECTION 6: MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
================================================================================

Every month / 100 km (whichever comes first):
- Check tire pressure (target: 45–50 PSI)
- Inspect brakes for responsiveness
- Clean scooter deck with a dry or slightly damp cloth
- Check all bolts on the stem and handlebar (target torque: 8–10 Nm)

Every 3 months / 500 km:
- Inspect brake pad thickness (replace if below 1.5 mm)
- Lubricate the folding mechanism with light machine oil
- Check wheel bearings for play or roughness
- Update firmware via the EcoRide app if prompted

Annually:
- Professional service recommended at an authorised EcoRide service centre
- Full brake bleed and pad replacement (if worn)
- Battery capacity diagnostic via the app (tap Diagnostics > Battery Health)

Cleaning Notes:
- The EcoSport X is rated IP54 — it can handle light rain and splashes.
- Never use a pressure washer or submerge any part of the scooter.
- Avoid spraying water directly at the charging port, display, or motor housing.

SECTION 7: SAFETY & COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

- Always wear a helmet. EcoRide strongly recommends CE-certified helmets.
- The EcoSport X is classified as a Personal Light Electric Vehicle (PLEV).
  Riders must comply with local road laws, which may restrict use on pavements or
  require registration in certain regions.
- Maximum rider age: 16 years and above.
- Do not carry passengers. The scooter is designed for a single rider only.
- Do not modify the scooter's firmware, motor controller, or battery. Doing so voids
  the warranty and may create safety hazards.
- In the event of a collision or significant impact, retire the scooter from use and
  have the frame inspected by an authorised service centre before riding again.
